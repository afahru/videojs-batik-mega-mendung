Batik Mega Mendung

A Video.js 10 skin where the timeline is a wave. The progress bar is a mega mendung cloud wave that fills with gold as the story moves, the play button is a medallion, and the control bar is deep indigo with a batik cloud pattern. Every control is real: play, seek, volume, speed, fullscreen.

 /

Click the video to play or pause. Double-click left or right to seek 10 seconds, double-click center for fullscreen. Keys: Space play and pause, M mute, F fullscreen, Left and Right seek 5 seconds.

Wave timeline

The seek bar is a mega mendung cloud wave. Gold fills the wave as the story moves, and the thumb is a small gold medallion.

Medallion play

A large gold ring medallion sits at the center before playback starts, with a slow breathing glow that stops the moment you press play.

Indigo and gold

Deep indigo control bar with a faint batik cloud pattern, gold icons, cream time readout. Built on Video.js 10 web components, so every control is native and keyboard friendly.
